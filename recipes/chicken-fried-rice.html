Chicken Fried Rice Recipe

Description

A regular dish of chicken fried rice, can be served with veg & shrimp. Very quick, perfect for a meal that requires not much effort to make.

Ingredients

  • Basmati White Rice
  • Chicken
  • Light Soy Sauce
  • x2 Eggs

Steps

  1. Begin cooking rice with boiling water, either through a rice cooker or pot
  2. Dice up chicken
  3. Optional: Season chicken, I personally use salt & pepper
  4. Cook chicken in wok at medium heat, shake ocasionally to prevent sticking
  5. Once rice is finished, beat two eggs into wok and stir into scrambled egg
  6. Once eggs are scrambled, put cooked rice in wok
  7. Add light soy sauce to rice and shake so that the sauce is consistent across the rice
  8. All done. Dish it out and enjoy!
